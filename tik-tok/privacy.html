Privacy Policy — AI Film Studio

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What the app is

AI Film Studio is a personal desktop application used by a single author to publish videos of their own animated films to their own social media accounts. It runs locally on the author's computer. There is no server that stores user data.

Data we access

When the author connects their own TikTok account, the app receives from TikTok: the basic profile of that account (open ID, display name, avatar) and access tokens that allow it to upload videos chosen by the author.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the author's videos to the author's account and to show the author which account is connected. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

Where the data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a local file on the author's computer. They are never sent to any third party other than TikTok's own API.

Other people's data

The app does not collect, store, or process data of any other TikTok users.

Deleting data

The author can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file. After that the app keeps no data from TikTok.
